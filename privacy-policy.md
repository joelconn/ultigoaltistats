# Privacy Policy for UltiGoaltiStats

**Last updated: September 27, 2026**

UltiGoaltiStats is an iOS app for tracking ultimate frisbee and goaltimate stats. It is developed by Joel Conn, an individual developer ("I", "me"). This policy explains what information the app handles, where it goes, and how you control it.

**The short version:** I don't collect any of your data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. Your data stays on your device unless you choose to export it to your own Google or GitHub account.

## Information stored on your device

The app stores the following on your iPhone or iPad:

- Team names, tournament names and opponent names
- Player rosters: names, jersey numbers, gender (used for mixed-division lines) and line assignments
- Game records: play-by-play events, scores and calculated statistics

This data is never sent to me or to any server I operate. Keep in mind that rosters usually include other people's names (your teammates). Please only enter information you have their permission to record.

## Optional features that send data to your own accounts

The app has two optional export features. Neither is turned on by default, and each one runs only when you use it.

### Google Sheets export

If you sign in with Google and export a game or a team's lifetime stats, the app creates a new spreadsheet in **your** Google account. The spreadsheet contains the team and opponent names, the game date, score and tournament, and each player's name, jersey number and statistics.

To do this, the app:

- Asks Google for permission to create and edit spreadsheets in your account (the Google Sheets scope). It does not request access to your Gmail, Contacts or Google Drive files.
- Stores the sign-in tokens Google returns, plus your Google account email address (so the app can show which account you're signed in with), on your device.
- Talks directly to Google. None of this data passes through me, and I can't see your spreadsheets.

You can sign out of Google in the app at any time, which removes the stored tokens and email from your device. You can also revoke the app's access at [myaccount.google.com/permissions](https://myaccount.google.com/permissions). Spreadsheets already created stay in your Google account until you delete them. Your use of Google services is governed by [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

### Publishing stats to GitHub Pages

If you set up GitHub publishing, the app uploads a stats web page to a GitHub repository you choose. GitHub Pages then serves it as a **public website**. Anyone with the link can see it, and it may be indexed by search engines. The published page includes player names and statistics.

To do this, the app:

- Uses a GitHub personal access token that you create and paste in, along with your repository owner, repository name and branch.
- Stores these settings in the iOS Keychain. If iCloud Keychain is turned on, they sync to your other Apple devices through your iCloud account.
- Talks directly to GitHub. I don't receive your token or your data.

**Please get your teammates' OK before publishing their names and stats publicly.** To stop publishing, clear the token in the app's GitHub settings and delete or revoke the token at [github.com/settings/tokens](https://github.com/settings/tokens). Pages you've already published stay online until you remove them from your repository. Your use of GitHub is governed by the [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

### Sharing and exporting files

If you use the iOS share sheet to send stats to another app (such as Messages, Mail or Files), that app receives only what you chose to share.

## What the app does not do

- It does not collect data for me or send data to any server I operate.
- It does not use analytics, advertising or tracking tools, and does not track you across other apps or websites.
- It does not access your location, contacts, photos or microphone.
- It does not sell or share your data with anyone.

## Keeping and deleting your data

Your data stays on your device for as long as you keep it. You can delete teams, players and games inside the app. Deleting the app removes its data from your device. It does not remove:

- Spreadsheets already created in your Google account
- Pages already published to your GitHub repository
- GitHub settings stored in iCloud Keychain (clear these in the app before deleting it, or remove them in the Passwords app)

Because I never receive your data, there is nothing for me to delete on my end. If you have questions, email me.

## Children

UltiGoaltiStats is not directed at children under 13, and I don't knowingly collect personal information from anyone.
